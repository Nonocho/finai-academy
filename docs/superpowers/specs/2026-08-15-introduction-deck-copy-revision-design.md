# Introduction deck copy revision

## Objective

Make the introduction deck factual and professional while preserving its current visual system, slide sequence, timings, and technical content.

## Approved direction

- Replace promotional or slogan-like headings with descriptive teaching language.
- Keep the existing concise format and information density.
- Preserve the two-day schedule, capstone scope, provider strategy, and validation criteria.
- Use `First Finance - Arnaud Demes` as the footer on every slide that contains course chrome.
- Keep the title slide visually unchanged apart from factual copy where appropriate.

## Representative copy changes

- `The product comes first` becomes `Capstone project: Financial Analyst Copilot`.
- `Each failure creates the need for the next engineering layer` becomes `Technical progression of the application`.
- `Success means a working, inspectable and defensible copilot` becomes `Expected deliverables and validation criteria`.
- `Now build the first response` becomes `Next: Notebook 01 — Model Gateway`.

## Acceptance criteria

- No new slide and no deleted slide.
- No change to the visual identity or the teaching schedule.
- All visible copy is factual, concise, and suitable for a professional technical class.
- All repeated footers use the exact requested wording.
- The final deck renders without clipping, unintended overlap, or inconsistent page furniture.
