# 04 - Document ingestion and chunking

## Learning promise

Learners can design an ingestion pipeline that preserves the structure and meaning
of financial documents.

## Deck narrative

PDF reality → parsing → Markdown structure → tables → metadata → chunking
strategies → contextual enrichment → cost.

## Notebook lab

Compare fixed, recursive, heading-aware, and semantic chunks from the same report.

## Checkpoint

1. What information is lost by naive fixed-size chunking?
2. Why should metadata be attached during ingestion?
3. When is contextual enrichment worth its cost?
