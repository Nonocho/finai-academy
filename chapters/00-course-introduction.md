# Course introduction — AI Engineering for Asset Management

**FinAI Academy — Arnaud Demes**

## Purpose

This opening block shows the finished Financial Analyst Copilot before learners
build it. It establishes the product mission, the two-day learning sequence, the
evidence contract, and the boundary between notebooks and prebuilt application
plumbing.

## Learner outcome

By the end of the introduction, the learner can identify the role of the model,
prompt, context, retrieval layer, tools, workflow, agent, MCP server, tracing,
and evaluation inside the final application.

## Product mission

The capstone compares NVIDIA and Schneider Electric using official documents,
structured financial data, deterministic calculations, and recent news. Every
material claim must retain its evidence and be labelled as a reported fact,
calculation, management claim, external fact, interpretation, or open question.

## Teaching rhythm

Every lesson follows the same sequence:

1. understand the business or engineering problem;
2. build the smallest working version;
3. expose a controlled failure;
4. add the engineering improvement;
5. verify the result; and
6. integrate the stable component into the capstone.

## Delivery

Day 1 builds an evaluated financial RAG pipeline. Day 2 turns that pipeline into
a stateful application with typed tools, a bounded agent, MCP interoperability,
and trajectory evaluation.
